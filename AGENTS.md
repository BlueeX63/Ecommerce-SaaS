Design a premium, modern 3D ecommerce landing page for a luxury sneaker brand. The hero section should feature a highly realistic 3D shoe floating in the center with soft ambient lighting, realistic shadows, and subtle idle animation. As the user scrolls down, the shoe should remain the main focus, slowly rotating on all three axes while smoothly moving downward with the scroll (scroll-linked animation). The transition must feel cinematic and physically smooth using GSAP ScrollTrigger or React Three Fiber. As the scroll reaches the product section, the 3D shoe should seamlessly shrink, align itself, and settle perfectly into a premium product card as the featured product image. Once it locks into the card, the card reveals the product name, price, rating, available sizes, color swatches, and an "Add to Cart" button with elegant fade and slide animations. The entire interaction should feel like one continuous transformation rather than separate scenes. Use a clean luxury aesthetic with plenty of whitespace, glassmorphism accents, smooth micro-interactions, and premium typography. Maintain 60 FPS performance, responsive behavior across devices, and realistic PBR materials for the shoe.
The shoe should cast dynamic shadows, react naturally to camera movement, and have subtle reflections. During the scroll transition, the camera should gently orbit around the shoe while the background changes smoothly, creating the feeling that the product is being showcased in a high-end Apple or Nike product presentation. The animation should use easing, motion blur, and depth effects to create a premium experience. The final product card should appear as though the shoe naturally docks into its designated position rather than abruptly changing.
